About US United Trucking

US United Trucking Inc is a Southern California trucking company specializing in port drayage and container transportation. Our mixed fleet of Day Cab and Sleeper tractors supports port moves, local deliveries and warehouse locations where maneuverability matters. We focus on responsive communication, dependable scheduling and efficient container movement for shippers, brokers, warehouses and logistics partners.
